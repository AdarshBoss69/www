Laser Hair Removal

Painless, permanent hair reduction using US-FDA approved Diode technology.

About the Treatment

Our clinic uses the latest Diode Laser technology, which is the gold standard for hair removal on Indian skin types. The laser targets the melanin in the hair follicle, heating it up and destroying the root without damaging the surrounding skin. It helps reduce hair growth significantly over a course of 6-8 sessions.

Benefits

  • Permanent reduction in hair density.
  • Prevents ingrown hairs.
  • Softer, smoother skin texture.
  • Painless procedure with cooling tip.

Pre-Care Instructions

  • Shave the area 24 hours before appointment.
  • Avoid waxing or plucking for 3 weeks prior.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure/tanning.

Acne Scar Revision

Restore your skin's texture with advanced resurfacing techniques.

Treatment Approach

Acne scars require a multi-modal approach. Dr. Mukta evaluates the type of scars (ice-pick, boxcar, or rolling) and customizes a plan. We combine MnRF (Microneedling Radiofrequency), Fractional CO2 Laser, and Subcision to break down scar tissue and stimulate new collagen production.

Technologies Used

  • MnRF: For deep collagen remodeling.
  • CO2 Fractional Laser: For surface resurfacing.
  • Chemical Peels: For pigmentation issues.

Anti-Aging & Peels

Turn back time with medical-grade skin rejuvenation.

Why Medical Peels?

Unlike salon facials, medical chemical peels penetrate deeper layers of the skin to exfoliate dead cells, reduce pigmentation, and stimulate cell turnover. We use safe, dermatologist-tested peels (Glycolic, Salicylic, Yellow Peel) tailored to your skin concern.
